🎮 Game Prompt: Aetherra: Last Spark

Game Type:
2D side-scrolling retro platformer inspired by classics like Super Mario Bros.

Platform:
Browser-based (HTML5 / Canvas / WebGL)

Works on desktop (keyboard) and mobile (touch controls)
Fully client-side (no server required)
🌍 Core Concept

In the polluted world of Aetherra, players take on the role of a young explorer determined to restore balance by collecting discarded batteries and bringing them back into the energy cycle.

The game combines:

Platforming (jump, run, avoid hazards)
Exploration
Light puzzle mechanics
Environmental restoration system
🕹️ Controls

Desktop:

⬅️ / ➡️ Arrow Keys → Move left/right
SPACE → Jump
(Optional) SHIFT → Sprint

Mobile:

Left side: ⬅️ ➡️ touch buttons
Right side: ⬆️ Jump button
Responsive UI overlay
🎨 Visual Style
Pixel-art, retro 16-bit aesthetic
Bright but slightly desaturated palette (to reflect pollution vs restoration)
Smooth parallax scrolling backgrounds
Animated tiles (water, fog, toxic zones)
Before/after environmental transformation visuals
🌱 Story Summary

Aetherra was once a perfect balance of nature and technology. Over time, discarded batteries poisoned the land.

You discover a hidden message:

Batteries are not waste—they can be reused.

You begin a journey across corrupted regions to collect batteries and restore life.

🧩 Gameplay Features
1. Platforming Mechanics
Running, jumping, gravity physics
Moving platforms
Breakable blocks
Hidden areas
2. Battery Collection System
Collectible items: 🔋 batteries
Each battery contributes to:
Level restoration
Score
Ending outcome
3. Environmental Transformation (Core Mechanic)
As player collects batteries:
Toxic fog disappears
Water becomes clear
Plants regrow
Wildlife returns
4. Hazards
Toxic spills (damage over time)
Polluted water
Mutated creatures
Falling debris
5. Light Puzzle Elements
Activate generators
Restore power circuits
Move objects to unlock paths
🗺️ Levels / Worlds
🏙️ Abandoned City
Rooftop jumping
Mechanical puzzles
Broken elevators
🌲 Polluted Forest
Toxic zones
Animal rescue interactions
Hidden safe paths
🏭 Industrial Ruins
Conveyor belts
Dark corridors
Energy systems to repair
🕳️ Underground Tunnels
Tight platforming
Limited visibility
Environmental hazards
👥 Characters
Player: customizable (boy/girl explorer)
Helpers: NPCs giving hints, tools, upgrades
Opposers: characters who believe it's too late
⚡ Progression System
Batteries = progress currency
Unlock:
New areas
Abilities (double jump, resistance suit)
Visual world restoration
🏁 Endings (Dynamic Outcome)
🌿 Good Ending
Most batteries collected
World fully restored
🌫️ Partial Ending
Some regions remain polluted
⚠️ Bad Ending
Heavy environmental damage persists
💡 Core Message

Batteries are not trash — they are a responsibility.
